- **Enemy overworld sets:** none missing.   The path that works at this size is the hand-authored grid approach used for Taren, `party_kit.py`
  and now `src/enemy_ow_remaining.py`: fix the proportions first, then dress the silhouette. A
  palette-sampling generator was tried twice and discarded before Batch 16.
  **Stand-ins still in use:** none for the named cast. Vell and Nessa got dedicated sets in
  batch 15; Berrin, the three Chapter 8 ward sentinels, the Cinderhollow burners and the Lost
  Ones have theirs from batches 13 and 14.
