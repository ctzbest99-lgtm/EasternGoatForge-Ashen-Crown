## Known art gaps (measured 2026-11-16)

- **Enemy overworld sets:** 18 enemy kinds have battle art but no 16x24 map set — `bandit_veteran`, `briar_hulk`, `crown_crossbowman`, `crown_patrolman`, `cutpurse`, `dock_thug`, `garrison_field_surgeon`, `garrison_pikeman`, `glyph_wisp`, `grey_ash_wolf`, `lost_one`, `night_moth_swarm`, `occupation_soldier`, `sanctuary_warden`, `shade_stalker`, `spiderling`, `ward_sentinel`, `fallen_knight`.
  (Measured by counting `spr_<kind>_ow16_*` resources against `spr_<kind>_idle_strip4`. Boss-phase
  battle art such as `blackguard_p2`/`p3` is excluded: those never stand on a map.)
  A procedural generator was built and tried twice for these and **did not meet the bar** (flat
  torsos, featureless heads, unreadable silhouettes next to the hand-drawn `bandit_ow.py` /
  `ash_wolf` sets), so it was discarded rather than imported. The path that works at this size is
  the hand-authored grid approach used for Taren and now by `party_kit.py`: fix the proportions
  first (hair cap, eye band, shoulders, belt, gapped legs, boots), then dress the silhouette.
- **Named speakers without art:** Alden (0 sprite resources anywhere), Nim and Derry (no portrait).
- **No left-facing battle strips:** the party is flipped with `image_xscale`.
