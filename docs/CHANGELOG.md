## Unreleased — Batch 7–8 enemy template rebalance (`feature/template-rebalance`)
- `scr_enemy_scaled` retuned after simulating the Chapter 3–5 fights: HP **+9 per level** (was +7), ATK **+3 per level** (was +2), boss HP **× 1.2 × expected party size** (was ×1.5) with the same +3 ATK / DEF, and a new `atk_add` per-enemy extra. Elites, SPD, MDEF, XP and gold are unchanged.
- Why: the party gains +2 DEF and +2 ATK per level plus a fourth member, so the old curve fell behind — random fights were 100% wins at 87–98% party HP left, and bosses could not kill anyone (Commander Hailen was 100% at every level with 58–70% HP left).
- Effect on the simulated fights: Sanctuary Warden 83/98/100% → **27/61/89%** at levels 13/14/15; Hailen 100% everywhere → **90/99/100%** at 16/17/18 with 27–48% party HP left; Debtman 90/100/100% → **71/99/100%** at 10/11/12; random battles now cost 20–25% of party HP at the bottom of a band.
- **Commander Hailen** carries the only `atk_add` (+10), because the flat boss bonus left him long but harmless.
- `tools/balance_sim.py` extended through Chapter 5 (magic damage vs MDEF, two-phase bosses, the Field Surgeon's heal, plus crits / Last Stand / bleed / Slow / Collect / stances / yields); it prints the tables recorded in `docs/DECISIONS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
- `gamemaker/` re-synced by copying the generated script; the generator's per-frame UUID churn was **not** carried into this change (re-running `gen_gamemaker.py` writes fresh frame file names and produces a ~6,000-file diff).
- XP and gold per enemy were left alone, so the harder fights currently pay the same as before — listed as an open item in DECISIONS.
- Nothing has been compiled or playtested in GameMaker.
