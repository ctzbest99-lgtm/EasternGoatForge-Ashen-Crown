# Art Direction

Pixel art with classic top-down JRPG readability, dark medieval fantasy atmosphere, and strong silhouettes. This is not a side-view game.

- Top-down four-direction sprites.
- Requested player reference: 32×48 pixels; final frame layout and palette need confirmation.
- Consistent sprite origins, collision masks, palette, and animation timing.
- Room resolution and tile scale remain undecided.

Requested but not included: Kael sprite/template and numbered X/Y guide; Bandit and Fallen Knight sprites; rare encounter and Zone 1 boss sprites; tileset, UI, music, and sound. Do not claim image files exist unless added to the repository.
