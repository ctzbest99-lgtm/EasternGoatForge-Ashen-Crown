# Art Direction

Pixel art with classic top-down JRPG readability, dark medieval fantasy atmosphere, and strong silhouettes. This is not a side-view game.

- Top-down four-direction sprites.
- Requested player reference: 32×48 pixels; final frame layout and palette need confirmation.
- Consistent sprite origins, collision masks, palette, and animation timing.
- Room resolution and tile scale remain undecided.

Model sheets (proportions, palettes, frames, import settings) are specified in `docs/CHARACTER_MODELS.md`. The sheets in `assets/characters/` are script-generated placeholders for testing movement — they are not final art and do not replace a pixel artist's pass.

Final art requested but not included: Kael sprite/template and numbered X/Y guide; Bandit and Fallen Knight art (placeholder sheets exist, see the inventory); rare encounter and Zone 1 boss sprites; tileset, UI, music, and sound. Do not claim image files exist unless added to the repository.
